# Provisional Earnings

**Tabulates the "provisional earnings" disclosures companies file before quarterly results are finalized.** Revenue, operating profit, net income, and YoY changes — straight from the filing's own table. Use it for earnings-season quick checks.

## What it answers

- **Provisional revenue / operating profit / net income** with **year-over-year changes** — shows the filing's original table and structures the headline numbers.
- States the **consolidated/separate basis** and units alongside.
- Absorbs non-financial provisional filings too — automakers' vehicle sales, food companies' regional revenue — formats differ, but the original table comes through as markdown.
- Widen the window and it collects several quarters of provisional filings in order.
- The [market-wide disclosure digest](../../../wiki/tools/screener.md) also surfaces provisional-earnings cards, so a morning routine shows "who reported provisional numbers today" at a glance.
- Source: DART material disclosures (provisional earnings, fair-disclosure). Details → [provisional_earnings](../../../wiki/tools/provisional_earnings.md).
