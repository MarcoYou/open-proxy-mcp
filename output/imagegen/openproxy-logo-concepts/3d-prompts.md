## 05 — Glass lattice

Primary request: Create one premium three-dimensional geometric symbol where scattered data pieces gather to form an open O.

## 06 — Metal modules

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: OpenProxy brand-symbol exploration
Primary request: Create one premium 3D geometric logo symbol where small data units assemble into an incomplete O, deliberately different from glass cells.
Scene/backdrop: pure white seamless studio background, no mockup.
Subject: A sculptural open torus built from 14 precisely aligned matte anodized metal modules, each a low rectangular wedge with chamfered facets and narrow gaps. The assembled lower-right three quarters form a rigorous angular ring with a clean central opening. At the upper-left, the construction dissolves into 8 loose geometric modules: small cubes, short bars and square plates, each positioned on the same radial system so the assembly logic is obvious. Some approaching modules are partially transformed from tiny pixel-like blocks into the larger wedge modules. Use intentional modular joints, not an organic curve.
Style/medium: abstract 3D industrial design object, Swiss/Japanese product-design discipline, premium and understated.
Color palette: mineral tones only: satin graphite, titanium gray, brushed aluminum and one or two muted cobalt-blue module faces. No orange, red, gold, warm wood or saturated rainbow.
Avoid: transparent glass, hollow wireframe, generic circular ribbon, letter O typography, infinity, power button, spinning loader, globe, coins, arrows, checks, gear teeth, dramatic sci-fi glow, UI, captions, watermark.
```
