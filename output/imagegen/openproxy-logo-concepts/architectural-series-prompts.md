# OpenProxy architectural logo series

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Shared visual criteria: institutional trust, weight, precision, matte graphite metal, near-front view, and a restrained neutral studio background.

## A — Open Colonnade

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: OpenProxy 3D symbol exploration. Brand traits: institutional trust, weight, precision, transparency, evidence-based judgment. Create one original abstract architectural mark, centered on a square soft off-white studio background, nearly front-facing with shallow isometric depth. Dense matte graphite anodized metal with restrained cool slate highlights, crisp machined edges, narrow exact seams, a low stable center of gravity, controlled soft light and a compact contact shadow. Symbol only: no wordmark, no letters, no text. Simple enough to reduce into a flat vector later and remain recognizable at 32px. Avoid circles, rings, classical ornament, literal courthouse illustration, law-firm clip art, bank logo clichés, scales of justice, shields, checkmarks, arrows, globes, eyes, crypto/web3 styling, bright colors, glass, glossy chrome, captions, watermark.
Concept A — Open Colonnade. Abstract the structural logic of a Parthenon into a modern computational symbol. Three broad square-prism columns stand on one thick stepped foundation and support one precise horizontal lintel. The center spacing is wider, creating a clear open portal. Each column is subtly built from three aligned vertical planes that converge into a single solid capital beneath the lintel, suggesting complex disclosures becoming a stable judgment. The lintel has one deliberate narrow opening at its upper-right end to convey openness. Use strict proportions, slight entasis expressed through faceted taper, and absolutely no triangular pediment or fluting. It should feel like a timeless institution redesigned by an industrial designer.
```
