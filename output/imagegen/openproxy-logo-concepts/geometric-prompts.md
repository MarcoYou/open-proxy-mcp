## Lattice ring — 04-geometric-lattice.png

Use case: logo-brand. Generate a sophisticated original geometric logo exploration for OpenProxy on pure white square canvas. Must look clearly different from a faceted solid ring: this concept is a geometric wireframe lattice converging into a dimensional open torus. Build one compact circular symbol from a disciplined hexagonal/triangular lattice in slight isometric projection. Left upper sector has 8-12 scattered small circular nodes with partial straight line connections, progressively joining a tight mesh toward the right and bottom, where thicker charcoal segments and several muted rust orange triangular cells establish a solid coherent structure. Preserve a substantial central white opening and a visible break in the upper left; overall reads as an open O formed by gathering data, not a ball. Use about 30-45 structural line segments maximum so it remains a designed logo, not a scientific visualization. Thick crisp medium-weight strokes, selectively filled geometric cells, varied density intentional; strong silhouette, mathematical rhythm and depth arising from geometry only. Charcoal #25272B and muted dark orange #B95429, tiny restrained gray accents. No shaded crescent, no three-dot loading spinner, no globe latitude/longitude grid, no literal arrow, no shield/checkmark, no braided knot, no 3D material rendering or lighting or shadow or gradient. Below mark use exact text "OpenProxy" in refined neutral sans-serif smaller than symbol. Generous white space. No other text. Professional architectural computational brand mark.
