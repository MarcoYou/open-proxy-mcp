# OpenProxy bright finance series

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Shared visual criteria: bright ivory, deep navy, brushed silver, measured financial geometry, institutional trust, and precise construction.

## A — Market Colonnade

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: OpenProxy 3D logo concept. The symbol must convey finance, institutional trust, weight, and exact analysis without using currency symbols or literal chart graphics. Create one compact geometric object, centered on a square bright warm-white studio background, nearly front-facing with shallow depth. Use luminous ivory mineral ceramic for main faces, deep desaturated navy for structural sides, brushed silver for precision seams, and at most one very small muted brass accent. Bright high-key lighting, soft short contact shadow, no black mass. Clean financial-editorial aesthetic, refined asset-management brand rather than a law firm or government seal. Symbol only, no wordmark, letters, numbers or text. Must simplify into a strong flat mark and remain readable at 32px. Avoid circles, shields, scales of justice, courthouse clip art, currency signs, literal trend arrows, candlestick charts, coins, globes, eyes, crypto/web3 styling, childish puzzle pieces, bright primary colors, neon, glass, captions, watermark.
Concept A — Market Colonnade. Abstract classical financial architecture and an ascending data series into one unified structure. Four wide rectangular columns stand on one low continuous base; their heights rise in four exact measured steps from left to right. A single thin lintel follows the same stepped progression across their tops, locking the columns into one compact silhouette while leaving three clean vertical openings. Use precise equal spacing and consistent ratios. Each ivory column has a narrow navy side face and a single fine silver datum line at the same height, suggesting comparable financial statements. The result should feel like a modern exchange building reduced to a disciplined geometric mark, not a literal temple.
```
